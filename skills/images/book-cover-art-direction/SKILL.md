---
name: book-cover-art-direction
description: Turn inspected competitor covers and a book brief into distinct cover design specifications, select a candidate, and review actual renders.
---

Start each book from its audience, promise, trim, exact copy, and verified genre references. Record explicit brand requirements separately from preferences expressed on earlier samples. Treat earlier palettes and layouts as evidence only when they address this book's brief.

For each comparable cover, record its edition and image reference. Inspect the full cover and a 160 px rendering. Separate visible observations from interpretation. Record which text reads first, how the image signals subject and audience, what detail disappears, and which visual choices are characteristic enough to avoid repeating.

Produce three distinct directions when the evidence supports them. Change the visual concept, hierarchy, and composition across candidates. For every direction supply:

- The visual promise and one detail that signals the subject even when the title is hidden.
- The evidence behind the decisions and how the candidate differs from the reference works.
- A layout specification with background, foreground and accent colors, available font choices, weight, title line breaks, relative sizes, alignment, spacing, and image placement.
- An artwork brief that describes the subject, geometry, materials, and light. Keep cover copy and font instructions in the layout specification.

Reject directions with weak subject recognition, close similarity to a reference, or an unclear title hierarchy. Rank the rest against audience fit, distinctiveness, and likely thumbnail performance. Under delegated selection, record the deciding evidence and choose a candidate. If none passes, return a revision brief.

Render and compare candidates at full size and 160 px before committing to a cover. Review the actual pixels for title readability, relevance of the image, and unwanted text. Verify print resolution and overflow separately. If the candidate fails, identify the failing element and revise the specification before another generation.

## Method references

- [Publication-led Design](https://github.com/ruyuezhen/publication-led-design) for starting from inspectable evidence, exploring distinct directions, and separating visual review from technical checks.
- [Amir Design Judgment by Amir Mušić](https://github.com/amirmushichge/amir-design-judgment) for linking a visible detail to its effect and a concrete revision. This book-specific workflow adapts that critique approach.
- [Cover Maker](https://github.com/Mikefluff/skills/blob/main/skills/cover-maker/SKILL.md) for medium-specific cover conventions and comparison of variants.
